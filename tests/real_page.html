International TT Series

Table Tennis Series is a new project on the international table tennis map. In 100 two-day tournaments, the best players in Europe compete for prize money and fame. To the TT Series tournaments, the organizers invite players playing in the highest league in various countries, but also players of the younger generation representing their countries on the international arena, who do not have the opportunity to compete with other players on a daily basis. The mixture of experience of famous and titled players as well as youthful fantasy and the will to achieve success of “underdoogs” provide emotions at the highest sports level. Each tournament is also an opportunity to present your skills on the world stage and a chance to sign a professional contract. Each match is broadcast via the streaming platform. The venue is the Sports Hall Complex “Czestochowa” – the largest sports complex in the region with a main arena with 7,000 seats.
